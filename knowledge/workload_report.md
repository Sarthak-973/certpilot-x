# Workload Report

Synthetic workforce analysis for CertPilot X.

## Observations
- Employees with more than 20 meeting hours show lower certification success.
- Best performance appears when meeting hours stay in the 12-18 range.
- Focus time above 15 hours tends to improve readiness for the study plan.

## Recommendation
- Schedule study blocks during low-meeting periods and keep weekly study cadence steady.
